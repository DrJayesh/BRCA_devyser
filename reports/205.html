Variant interpretation and clinical correlation:
The NM_001406720.1(BRCA2):​c.8954-1G>A variant causes a splice acceptor, intron change involving the alteration of a conserved nucleotide. The variant allele was found at a frequency of 0.00000205 in 1,460,084 control chromosomes in the GnomAD database, with no homozygous occurrence. In-silico tool predicts a pathogenic outcome for this variant. 1/1 splice prediction tools predict no significant impact on normal splicing. Variant has been reported in ClinVar as Likely pathogenic (★★).

Methodology:
DNA was extracted from whole blood using a commercial kit and was quantified on a fluorometer. Adequate DNA was used to make sequencing ready libraries using a CE-IVD marked commercial library preparation kit. The prepared libraries were quantified and their library size checked on a fragment analyser. QC passed libraries were sequenced on massively parallel sequencing platform to produce 150-basepair, paired end, Q30 raw sequencing data. Bioinformatics analysis including alignment of reads to human reference genome, quality filtering, variant calling and variant annotation was done using a proprietary software supplied along with the library preparation kit. SNVs, indels and CNVs detected were reported according to the ACMG guidelines for germline variants(PMID: 25741868) The output sequences are aligned to the human reference genome hg19 (GRCh37). Variants are identified and interpreted using Ion Reporter Software. The identified variant(s) is(are) annotated according to HGVS sequence variant nomenclature. Multiple in-silico predictors, such as SIFT, PolyPhen, MutationTaster etc. are used for variant impact on the protein function. Population and literature databases such as dbSNP, Exome Aggregation Consortium (ExAC), genome Aggregation Database (gnomAD), ClinVar, HGMD and PubMed etc are used for variant summary and classification. Variants are labelled based on the American College of Medical Genetics (ACMG) recommendations for 5-tier variant classification system: Pathogenic, Likely Pathogenic, Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS), Likely Benign and Benign.
 
Limitations and disclaimer:
A negative result does not exclude a heritable cancer risk. This test detects only variants within the coding regions and intron-exon boundaries of BRCA1 and BRCA2. Certain structural changes (e.g., large deletions/duplications, regulatory region variants, or deep intronic variants) may not be identified, and precise breakpoints are not determined. Deletions or duplications of two or fewer exons are detected, though they may be missed. Rare sequence variations, pseudogenes, homologous regions, and technical limitations can also lead to diagnostic errors. Additionally, low-level mosaic or somatic variants, gene conversion events, complex inversions, translocations, mitochondrial DNA variants, or repeat expansions are not intended for detection by this assay. Clinical decisions should rely on the treating physician’s independent medical judgment and comprehensive clinical data, not solely on the results of this report. Accurate interpretation depends on a detailed clinical history; if unavailable, accuracy cannot be guaranteed. Variant classifications can change over time—contact Jaipur Molecular Lab for future updates.
